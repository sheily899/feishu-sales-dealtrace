> Closed-Won Handoff Summarizer  When a CRM Opportunity stage changes to Closed Won, start the handoff process for the delivery or implementation team.

## When it fires

**Detector:** Trigger if the user wants to create handoff summaries when deals close or transition from sales to implementation, customer success, or delivery teams.

**Signal keywords:** `closed won`, `handoff`, `won deal`, `implementation handoff`, `csm handoff`, `delivery handoff`, `deal handoff`, `post-sale`, `onboarding handoff`

## What it does

Closed-Won Handoff Summarizer

When a CRM Opportunity stage changes to Closed Won, start the handoff process for the delivery or implementation team.

Identify the Closed Won Opportunity using the CRM event data. Capture the opportunity ID, then pull all relevant details from your CRM, including:

Opportunity name, amount, term, close date, and account

Account Executive (AE) owner and assigned CSM owner

Primary contact and opportunity team members

Products or line items sold

Key custom fields such as implementation tier, region, and segment

Use the opportunity ID to find all associated conversations in your call recorder. If none are linked directly, look for conversations connected to the same account. From those conversations, extract:

Participants and their roles

Customer goals, needs, and pain points

Decisions made and commitments promised by the AE

Technical details, integrations, or dependencies discussed

Timelines, blockers, and any risk indicators

Combine this information into a concise, structured summary that gives the delivery team full context on what was sold, who was involved, and what happens next. If conversation data is missing, include practical guidance such as "review CRM notes" or "schedule a follow-up with AE."

Format the message exactly as follows:

:tada: NEW CLOSED WON HANDOFF
:receipt: DEAL OVERVIEW
Summarize the customer, what they purchased, and commercial details.

:busts_in_silhouette: KEY STAKEHOLDERS
List key roles: Champion, Decision Maker, Technical Contact, Account Executive, Implementation Lead. Include names and titles from your CRM or call recorder conversations.

:dart: GOALS AND SUCCESS CRITERIA
Summarize the customer's objectives and desired business outcomes.

:jigsaw: SCOPE AND INTEGRATIONS
Describe the products or services sold, systems or integrations involved, and any relevant timelines.

:handshake: COMMITMENTS OR SPECIAL TERMS
List key promises, delivery expectations, or non-standard agreements from the sales process.

:warning: RISKS AND WATCHOUTS
Highlight potential blockers, technical dependencies, or misalignments identified in conversations.

:rocket: NEXT STEPS
List 3–5 immediate actions needed for a successful kickoff, with owners and target dates if available.

:paperclip: RESOURCES
Include direct links to the CRM Opportunity, linked call recorder conversations, and any key attachments such as the proposal or SOW.

Add a footer line that reads:
Generated by GTM Superintelligence | Opportunity ID: [ID] | [Date and Time UTC]

Post the message in the correct handoff channel (e.g., #deal-handoffs or the account-specific channel). Mention the AE and CSM.

## Trigger

**Type:** CRM stage change — fires when an Opportunity's stage enters a **Closed-Won** stage (sales → delivery/CS handoff).

**Resolve stages first — don't hardcode "Closed Won":** every org names stages differently. Resolve this org's actual won stage(s) via [CRM stage discovery](../../docs/crm-stages.md) (`gtmsi crm-stages`) — e.g., Salesforce: `OpportunityStage` where `IsWon = true`; HubSpot: the deal pipeline stage with `metadata.probability = 1.0`.
